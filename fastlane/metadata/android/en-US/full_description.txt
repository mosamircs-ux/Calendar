Planora Calendar is a clean, fast, and customizable schedule & task planner designed to keep your days organized effortlessly.

KEY FEATURES:
• Multiple Calendar Views: Switch seamlessly between Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Monthly+Daily, Yearly, and Agenda Event List views.
• Events & Tasks Together: Create events and to-do tasks with flexible durations, multiple reminders, custom colors, and powerful recurrence rules.
• Seamless CalDAV Sync: Sync with Google Calendar, Outlook, Nextcloud, Exchange, and other CalDAV providers.
• Home Screen Widgets: Modern customizable Monthly, Event List, and Today's Date widgets.
• Birthdays, Anniversaries & Holidays: Automatically import contact birthdays/anniversaries and regional holidays.
• Import & Export (.ics): Back up and transfer events and tasks using standard .ics calendar files.
• Modern Indigo & Violet Design: Crisp typography, automatic Light/Dark mode support, and customizable color themes.
