Privacy Policy

Planora Calendar · Android app · Package com.planora.calendar
Effective date: November 19, 2026 · Last updated: November 19, 2026

Planora Calendar ("we", "our", or "us") is an Android calendar application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used and stored, and the choices you have. We designed Planora Calendar to keep your data on your device. We do not operate a server that collects your calendar data, and we do not require you to create an account.

Summary
  • Your events, tasks and reminders are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your calendar data with us or with third parties.
  • If you set up CalDAV sync, data travels directly between your device and the CalDAV server you choose.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect a device advertising identifier, and you control ad personalization through the in-app consent settings.

1. Information the App Accesses

Planora Calendar requests the following Android permissions. Each one is used only for the feature described.

PermissionWhy it is used
Calendar (read and write)To display, create, edit and delete events on your device calendar and in the app.
Contacts (read)To let you pick attendees for an event. Contacts are read on your device and are not uploaded.
NotificationsTo show event and task reminders.
Exact alarms, boot completed, wake lock, vibrateTo deliver reminders on time, including after your device restarts.
Internet and network stateTo sync with CalDAV servers you configure and to load advertisements.
Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID)Used by Google AdMob to serve ads. Governed by the consent choices described in Section 3.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's Settings. Revoking a permission may disable the related feature.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Calendar events, tasks, reminders, schedules, categories and related settings are stored locally in the app's database on your device. This data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Exported or imported .ics files are saved wherever you choose on your device. We do not receive copies of these files.

3. Advertising and Consent

Planora Calendar displays advertisements to support its development and maintenance. We use the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location derived from network information if permitted, app interaction data, diagnostics and crash information. It uses this information to deliver and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Ads privacy and terms and in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to show consent dialogs where required, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions with privacy laws.

Depending on your location and your choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can review or change your ad privacy preferences at any time in the app under Settings → About → Ad Privacy Preferences.

You can also limit ad personalization through your device settings (for example, by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android's Ads settings).

4. CalDAV and Cloud Synchronization

If you add a CalDAV account, the app syncs calendar data directly between your device and the CalDAV server you specify. We do not operate that server, and we do not receive, store or view your calendar data during sync. The privacy practices of your CalDAV provider apply to data stored on its server.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your calendar events, contacts, tasks or reminders. We do not require sign-in, and we do not operate analytics or tracking that sends your personal calendar content to our servers. We do not sell or rent personal information.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except as described in Section 3 (Google AdMob and UMP) and Section 4 (the CalDAV server you choose to connect), and as required by law.

7. Data Security

Your calendar data is stored on your device and is protected by your device's security features, such as screen lock and device encryption. Advertising and consent requests made to Google are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Please keep your device secured with a passcode or biometric lock.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Calendar data: kept on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's local data.
  • Data held by Google (advertising): managed under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Data on a CalDAV server: managed by your CalDAV provider. Delete it through that provider.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Revoke app permissions in Android Settings.
  • Change ad consent and personalization in the app under Settings → About → Ad Privacy Preferences.
  • Delete events and data in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you may have rights under the GDPR or similar laws, including access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection. Because we do not hold your personal data on our servers, most of these requests can be handled on your device. For requests about data held by Google, contact Google directly. You may also contact us as described below.
  • If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined by law, beyond the Google advertising described in Section 3.

10. Children's Privacy

Planora Calendar is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can address it.
